About

10+ years spent moving between the studio, the stage and the headset: building worlds that fashion and culture can actually walk into.

I'm a London-based Creative Director and Creative Technologist running the independent studio BRGB, working across large-scale installation, XR and real-time production. I was an early hire at Satore Studio, where I became Head of Creative R&D and helped grow the team from a handful of people to thirty-two, working on fashion week lighting and video for Prada, Hugo Boss and Moschino. Across both, I've directed spatial and social-first work for Adidas, Umbro and Marshmallow Laser Feast.

Alongside the studio, I'm Senior Lecturer in Games at the University of East London, teaching game art, immersive media and creative technology to the next set of people who'll build this stuff.
