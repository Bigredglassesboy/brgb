About

10+ years spent moving between the studio, the stage and the headset: building worlds that fashion and culture can actually walk into.

I'm a London-based Creative Director and Creative Technologist running the independent studio BRGB, working across large-scale installation, XR and real-time production. I was an early hire at Satore Studio, helping grow the team from a handful of people to thirty-two, and have directed spatial and social-first work for the likes of Adidas, Umbro, Moschino and Marshmallow Laser Feast.

Alongside the studio, I lecture in Digital Media at the University of East London, teaching game art, immersive media and creative technology to the next set of people who'll build this stuff.
